## Search & Seizure Breaches

### High Severity (typically warrant exclusion application)

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Evidence to Review |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **No warrant, no statutory power, no consent** | Search conducted without any visible authority | s14 S&S Act 2012; s21 NZBORA | Warrants file, officer statements, consent records |
| **Warrant issued without reasonable grounds** | Affidavit contains stale information, hearsay, or speculation | s46, s47 S&S Act 2012 | Warrant application/affidavit, supporting documentation |
| **Warrant lacking particularity** | Authorises broad categories ("any documents", "all electronic devices") without nexus to specific offence | s48 S&S Act 2012; R v Fraser | Warrant document itself |
| **Night search without authorisation** | Search executed between 9pm-6am without express warrant authorisation | s49 S&S Act 2012 | Warrant, execution records |
| **Excessive use of force** | Property damage beyond necessary, injury during entry | s49(2) S&S Act 2012; s21 NZBORA | Photos of premises post-search, medical records |
| **Seizure beyond warrant scope** | Items seized not described in warrant, no plain view justification | s55 S&S Act 2012; s21 NZBORA | Warrant, exhibit list, seizure records |
| **Failure to provide search inventory** | Occupier not given list of seized items | s59 S&S Act 2012 | Occupier statement, receipt records |
| **Search of third-party property** | Search extends to property of person not subject to warrant | s21 NZBORA | Warrant scope, ownership records |

### Medium Severity (may affect weight, require direction)

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Evidence to Review |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Delayed application for warrant** | Time gap between grounds arising and warrant application | s46 S&S Act 2012 | Timeline of investigation |
| **Informant not independent** | Officer applying for warrant is also investigating officer | Best practice, judicial scrutiny | Warrant application |
| **No announcement before entry** | Failure to announce presence and authority before forced entry | s49 S&S Act 2012 | Bodycam footage, occupier statement |
| **Prolonged search duration** | Search extends well beyond reasonable time for described items | Implied s48 requirement | Search logs, timeline |
| **Copying/data extraction at scene** | Forensic copying of devices without specific warrant authorisation | s48 S&S Act 2012 (particularity) | Warrant wording, forensic logs |

## Arrest & Custody Breaches

### High Severity

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Evidence to Review |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Arrest without reasonable grounds** | No articulable basis linking accused to offence | s22 NZBORA; s315 Crimes Act 1961 | Arrest circumstances, officer statement, information available at time |
| **Failure to inform of reason for arrest** | Accused not told why they are being arrested | s23(1)(a) NZBORA; s316 Crimes Act 1961 | Arrest records, custody record, ERISP |
| **Failure to inform of right to lawyer** | No caution, incomplete caution, or caution not in language understood | s23(1)(b), s23(2) NZBORA | ERISP/custody record, officer notes, language assessment |
| **Denial of prompt lawyer access** | Lawyer contacted but not permitted to attend; significant delay | s23(1)(b) NZBORA | Custody timeline, lawyer attendance records |
| **Interview before lawyer consultation** | Interview commenced while lawyer en route or before attempt to contact | s23(1)(c) NZBORA | Custody timeline, interview start time |
| **Failure to inform of right to silence** | Not cautioned that anything said may be used in evidence | s23(2) NZBORA | ERISP commencement, caution records |
| **Arrest of child without additional safeguards** | Person under 18 arrested without appropriate adult present | s23(1)(b) NZBORA; Police youth practice | Custody records, age verification |
| **Extended custody without charge** | Detained for extended period without being charged | s23(3) NZBORA | Arrest time, charge time, custody record |

### Medium Severity

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Evidence to Review |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Delayed recording of custody events** | Custody record gaps or retrospective entries | Police policy; s23 NZBORA | Custody record timestamps |
| **Inadequate welfare checks** | Insufficient checks on detained person's welfare | Police policy | Custody record |
| **Interview of intoxicated person** | Interview proceeds despite significant intoxication | s61 Evidence Act 2006 (reliability) | Custody record observations, ERISP assessment |
| **No interpreter arranged** | Accused with limited English interviewed without interpreter | s77 Evidence Act 2006; s23(2) NZBORA | Language assessment records |
| **No appropriate adult for vulnerable person** | Mental health issues, intellectual disability not accommodated | Police vulnerable persons policy | Risk assessment, medical records |

## Interview & Statement Breaches

### High Severity (mandatory exclusion likely if proven)

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Evidence to Review |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Oppression** | Threats of violence, threats regarding family/children, threats of extra charges | s61(1) Evidence Act 2006 | Full ERISP transcript, accused account, custody video |
| **Inducements** | Promises of bail, promises of lenient sentence, promises of no charge | s61(2) Evidence Act 2006 | Full ERISP transcript |
| **False representation by officer** | Officer lies about evidence (e.g., "your co-accused has confessed", "DNA matches") | s56 Evidence Act 2006 | ERISP, actual evidence existence |
| **Statement not voluntary** | Accused was drugged, severely sleep-deprived, or mentally incapacitated | s61 Evidence Act 2006 | Medical records, custody timeline, ERISP assessment |
| **Interview without caution after decision to charge** | Continued questioning after charging decision made | s23 NZBORA; Police policy | Charge time vs interview time |
| **Covert interrogation** | Questioning in vehicle or corridor not recorded as interview | s23(1)(c) NZBORA (right to have lawyer present) | Officer notes, accused account, any recording |

### Medium Severity (may affect weight)

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Evidence to Review |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Leading questions in interview** | Officer suggests facts rather than asking open questions | Best practice; credibility | ERISP transcript |
| **Failure to put specific allegations** | Officer does not adequately disclose what accused is responding to | Fairness | ERISP, charging document timing |
| **Multiple officers — tag team interviewing** | Rotating officers to extend interview pressure | Reliability concern | ERISP, custody record |
| **Interview breaks not provided** | No meal/bathroom breaks during extended interview | s23 NZBORA; Police policy | Custody record, ERISP duration |
| **ERISP malfunctions** | Recording gaps, poor audio quality | Reliability; Police policy | Technical records, officer notes of what was said |
| **Unsigned statement** | Written statement not signed by accused | Weight; authentication | Statement document |

## Disclosure Breaches

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Remedy |
|--------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **No initial disclosure** | Charging document provided but no summary of facts | s60 CPA 2011 | Formal request; case review adjournment |
| **Incomplete summary of facts** | SoF omits key events, lacks detail on accused's involvement | s60(1)(b) CPA 2011 | Further disclosure request |
| **No criminal record provided** | Defendant's record not disclosed despite existence | s60(1)(c) CPA 2011 | Request record; if not provided, cannot be relied on for sentence |
| **Witness statements withheld** | Crown intends to call witness but statement not provided | s60(1)(f), s68 CPA 2011 | Request; may support exclusion or adjournment |
| **Expert report not provided** | Crown relies on expert opinion but report not disclosed | s60(1)(d), s68 CPA 2011 | Cannot rely on opinion without disclosure |
| **Exhibit list incomplete** | Items referred to in SoF not listed as exhibits | s68 CPA 2011 | Request complete exhibit list and chain of custody |
| **Video/audio not provided** | Reference to recordings but copies not given | s60(1)(e), s68 CPA 2011 | Request copies; may prevent reliance at trial |
| **Late disclosure** | Material provided shortly before or during trial | s62 CPA 2011 | Adjournment application; costs; adverse inference direction |
| **Withheld material — no reasons** | Crown indicates material withheld but no s63 notice | s63 CPA 2011 | Request s63 reasons; court review application |
| **Incomplete s63 reasons** | Reasons given but insufficiently specific | s63 CPA 2011 | Challenge adequacy; seek court review |
| **Police notebook entries not disclosed** | Officer notes referenced but not provided | s61 CPA 2011 (all relevant material) | Request; may contain inconsistencies with SoF |
| **Informant material not addressed** | Evidence suggests informant involvement but no disclosure | s61, s63 CPA 2011 | Request disclosure or s63 determination |
| **Third-party material not facilitated** | Medical/CYFS records relevant but not obtained | s61 CPA 2011 | Defence obtain consent or seek production order |
| **Previous consistent statements** | Crown has earlier statements of witnesses not disclosed | s61 CPA 2011 (potential inconsistencies) | Request all prior statements |
| **Unused material** | Material gathered but Crown not relying on it | s61 CPA 2011 (may assist defence) | Request all unused material |
| **Digital forensic logs** | Device extraction done but logs not provided | s68 CPA 2011; reliability | Request extraction methodology and logs |
| **Editing history** | Video/audio evidence edited but original not provided | s61 CPA 2011 (completeness) | Request unedited originals |

## Charging & Court Process Breaches

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Remedy |
|--------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Defective charging document** | Missing essential elements, wrong section cited, no date/location | s14-17 CPA 2011 | Challenge validity; request amendment |
| **Wrong offence category** | Category 2 charged as Category 3 (affects election) | s6 CPA 2011 | Seek reclassification |
| **Duplicitous charging** | One charge covers multiple distinct incidents | s17 CPA 2011 | Application to split/amend |
| **Delay in filing charge** | Significant gap between offence and charging | s24(c) NZBORA; abuse of process | Stay application |
| **Bail conditions excessive** | Conditions not justified by risk factors | s30 Bail Act 2000 | Variation application |
| **Failure to bring before court promptly** | Extended police bail without court appearance | s21-25 CPA 2011; s23(3) NZBORA | Application to dismiss or expedite |
| **Incorrect court venue** | Filed in court without jurisdiction | s14-16 CPA 2011 | Challenge jurisdiction |
| **Multiple informations** | Same offence charged in multiple courts | s17 CPA 2011 (duplicity); double jeopardy (s25 NZBORA) | Application to withdraw/stay |

## Evidence Handling Breaches

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Consequence |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Chain of custody gap** | Exhibit handled by person not recorded; time gaps in records | Reliability; s68 Evidence Act | May challenge exhibit integrity |
| **Improper storage** | Biological exhibits not refrigerated; digital evidence not hashed/secured | Best practice; contamination risk | Degradation; challenge reliability |
| **No photograph before processing** | Item processed (e.g., fingerprint powder) before photographing original state | Best practice | Lost exculpatory detail |
| **Mixed exhibits** | Multiple items stored together, potential cross-contamination | Contamination risk | Challenge DNA/fingerprint evidence |
| **Delayed forensic analysis** | Significant delay between seizure and analysis | Staleness; degradation | Challenge reliability; delay arguments |
| **No independent sampling** | Sample taken by police not independently witnessed | Reliability | Challenge sample integrity |
| **Document alteration** | Statement dates changed, pages added/removed | Authenticity; s68 Evidence Act | Challenge admissibility |
| **No ERISP continuity** | Recording starts late or stops early | Completeness; s68 Evidence Act | Challenge voluntariness; missing context |

## Privacy & Surveillance Breaches

| Breach | Indicators | Legal Basis | Evidence to Review |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tracking device without warrant** | GPS or electronic tracking used without judicial authorisation | s68-73 S&S Act 2012; s21 NZBORA | Surveillance records, device data, warrant file |
| **Covert surveillance — private setting** | Hidden camera/audio in home, hotel room, or other private space | s21 NZBORA; Privacy Act 2020 | Surveillance authorisation, location classification |
| **Interception without authority** | Phone/email interception without interception warrant | s48-52 S&S Act 2012; s21 NZBORA | Communication records, warrant file |
| **Accessing cloud data without authorisation** | Downloading from iCloud, Google, etc. without warrant or consent | s21 NZBORA; s48 S&S Act 2012 | Data extraction logs, warrant scope |
| **Retention of data beyond necessity** | Personal data retained after investigation concluded | Privacy Act 2020 IPP 9; s22 Privacy Act | Data retention policy, destruction records |
| **Disclosure to third parties** | Investigation details shared with non-law enforcement without authority | Privacy Act 2020 IPP 11 | Communication records |
| **Facial recognition search without basis** | Photo submitted to facial recognition database without reasonable grounds | Privacy Act 2020; s21 NZBORA | Database query records, authorisation |
| **Social media monitoring without authority** | Covert monitoring of social media accounts without warrant | s21 NZBORA; Privacy Act 2020 | Investigation records, authorisation |
